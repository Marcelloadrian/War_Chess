How to play

Your turn

You have 5 moves per turn. Moving, attacking, building, planting a mine, boarding a boat or capturing each cost 1. The yellow dots show what is left. When the green End turn button lights up you are out of moves. The enemy then plays its 5. A round is your turn plus theirs.

Win

Capture or destroy the enemy HQ 🏛 by moving infantry onto it and pressing Capture node. Missions in the menu have their own goal (shown top right).

Supplies

Every unit eats food each round and moving burns resources by terrain: plains and forest burn food, ruins burn materials, mud burns fuel, sea burns money. When a unit's supply hits 0 it freezes, and any enemy with supplies beats a frozen unit instantly.

Payday

Every round you get a small trickle of supplies, and every 5 rounds a big payday. Only supply bases 📦 connected to your HQ by land add to it. Enemy units and the tiles next to them cut the road. Low morale shrinks income.

Capturing a base

Select infantry, walk onto the base, press Capture node. It gives +5 morale.

Fighting

Click an enemy next to your unit for the engage screen: force attack, capture, or bribe. Force odds are estimates; a scout nearby sharpens them. Bribes can backfire into a double agent. You cannot attack what the fog hides.

Mines and engineers

A planted mine 💣 kills everyone on its tile when you detonate it, including your own units. Engineers 🔧 defuse adjacent mines.

Boats

A transport ⛴️ carries 2 foot units. Walk next to it, press Board, sail, then Unload onto an adjacent land tile.

No undo

This is war: every move is final.

Supplies, payday and bankruptcy

Every round you also get a small trickle of every resource (scaled by morale, never by supply bases). Payday, every 5 rounds, is the big shipment and the only one your supply bases multiply. Enemy units and the 8 tiles around them cut your supply road. A unit freezes the moment any resource it lives on hits 0, and any enemy with supplies beats a frozen unit instantly. Bullets and bombs share one armory cap (200, minus 40 per barracks, minimum 50). Food, fuel and money forecasts are in the side panel.

Morale scales income (×0.3 at 0, ×1 at 50, ×1.3 at 100) and upkeep (×1.5 at 0, ×0.9 at 100). Under 30, supply bases give nothing. Morale caps the supply multiplier: ×1 at 30 (bases pay nothing), ×3 at 50, ×5 at 75, ×8 at 100, blended in between. Bases beyond the cap are wasted.

Combat

Strength = base × rank (+15% per rank) × health × morale × the defender's terrain (forest +25%, ruins +50%), each side randomly ±15%. Attacker at 1.0× or more wins, 0.7 to 1.0× is repelled with damage on both sides, under 0.7× the attacker is destroyed. Every 2nd kill promotes a unit (max rank 5). Each attack costs bullets. The engage screen shows only an estimate; a scout within 5 tiles sharpens it.

Capture, bribes, prisoners

Single capture: infantry only, at least 1.6× strength and the target at 60% HP or less. Surround: every open tile around an enemy is blocked and at least 2 enemies are adjacent; infantry are captured, other units destroyed. Bribe cost is 20 × rank × offer (1 to 3×). The odds compare your morale with the target side's: equal is neutral, 50 points ahead gives about ×1.5 success, 50 behind about ×0.5, and backfires get rarer the further ahead you are. Outcomes are defect, surrender, resisted or backfire (a double agent that leaks your mines and flips back after 3 rounds). Interrogating: question (costs money, prisoner lives, shows about half of enemy mines) or force (prisoner dies, shows all mines, −8 morale).

Outposts and attrition

Units more than 8 tiles from every finished HQ, Supply Base or Barracks do not freeze, but they cost 2.5× upkeep. A deep push without outposts bleeds your stock, so build bases as you advance. The goal card warns you when units are out of range. Buildings can never be built on water, even on ice.

Mines, boats, fog, weather

A mine costs 1 bomb and 2 materials and kills every unit on its tile when you detonate it, even yours. Engineers defuse mines on an adjacent tile. Transports carry 2 foot units: board when adjacent, unload onto an adjacent land tile. You see 3 tiles around units (scouts 6, HQ 4); enemies in forest or ruins are hidden beyond 2 tiles. Weather is re-rolled every 3 rounds (50% clear, 25% rain, 10% storm, 15% freeze). Rain turns plains into mud (slow, burns fuel). A freeze turns sea into ice that land units can walk across, but when the weather changes the ice melts and every land unit still standing on it drowns.

Turns

5 moves per turn for you and the enemy. Interrogating, releasing and ending the turn are free.

Play replay

A replay re-runs every action from the start of the game, showing what you saw (fog included). Your current game is not touched.

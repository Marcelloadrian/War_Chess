How to play

Your turn

You have 5 moves per turn. Moving, attacking, building, planting a mine, boarding a boat or capturing each cost 1. The yellow dots show what is left. When the green End turn button lights up you are out of moves. The enemy then plays its 5. A round is your turn plus theirs.

Win

Capture or destroy the enemy HQ 🏛 by moving infantry onto it and pressing Capture node. Missions in the menu have their own goal (shown top right).

Supplies

Every unit eats food each round and moving burns resources by terrain: plains and forest burn food, ruins burn materials, mud burns fuel, sea burns money. When a unit's supply hits 0 it freezes, and any enemy with supplies beats a frozen unit instantly.

Payday

Every 5 rounds you get new supplies. Only supply bases 📦 connected to your HQ by land add to it. Enemy units and the tiles next to them cut the road. Low morale shrinks income.

Capturing a base

Select infantry, walk onto the base, press Capture node. It gives +5 morale.

Fighting

Click an enemy next to your unit for the engage screen: force attack, capture, or bribe. Force odds are estimates; a scout nearby sharpens them. Bribes can backfire into a double agent. You cannot attack what the fog hides.

Mines and engineers

A planted mine 💣 kills everyone on its tile when you detonate it, including your own units. Engineers 🔧 defuse adjacent mines.

Boats

A transport ⛴️ carries 2 foot units. Walk next to it, press Board, sail, then Unload onto an adjacent land tile.

No undo

This is war: every move is final.

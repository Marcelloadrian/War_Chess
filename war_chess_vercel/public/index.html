📖 Field Manual

Terrain burns resources
Plains burn Food (little), Forest burns Food (lots), Urban burns Materials (tanks cannot enter), Mud burns Fuel, Sea burns Money (only ships and transports).
Weather
Changes every 3 rounds. Rain makes plains, mud and forest burn more; storms make sea trips and mud costly. Check the HUD before a flank.
Payday & supply lines
Every 5 rounds. Only supply bases connected to your HQ by land count (🔗 in the HUD). Enemy units, and the tiles next to them, cut the road. Low morale caps the bonus (nothing below 30) and shrinks income.
Bankruptcy
When a unit's lifeline resource hits 0 it freezes. Any enemy with resources > 0 beats a frozen unit instantly, ignoring rank and force.
Force vs money
Force compares Force Numbers (rank, hp, terrain, morale, ±15%). Bribes roll SSR defection / SR surrender / R resisted / SSR backfire (a double agent that leaks your mines). Higher offers help; frozen targets are cheap.
Mines & engineers
A planted bomb is hidden and kills EVERYONE on its tile when you detonate it, including your own units (extra morale loss). Engineers defuse adjacent mines, and probing an empty tile is free.
Veterancy
Every 2nd kill promotes a unit (max rank 5).
Morale
Falls with losses, force interrogation (−8) and friendly-fire. Rises with live captures, subversion and capturing nodes with infantry.
Fog of war
You only see tiles near your units and buildings (dark = unseen). Enemies in forest or urban tiles are hidden unless within 2 tiles. Scouts see 6 tiles. You cannot attack or bribe what you cannot see.
Naval pincer
A transport carries 2 infantry/engineers/scouts. Walk next to it, press Board, sail, then Unload onto an adjacent land tile. If the transport dies, so does its cargo.
Consequences
Wounded units heal slowly beside your own HQ, bases and barracks (if fed). Destroyed walls leave rubble (cover, no tanks). Veterans from a won campaign mission join the next one. There is no undo.
Intel is fuzzy
Engagement odds are estimates. A scout near the target sharpens them.
Enemy commanders
Balanced, Banker (bribes a lot), Brute (force-interrogates prisoners) or Flanker (lands troops from the sea).
